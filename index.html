The problem / Near-term coordination

The schedule isn’t the whole story.

Construction teams already plan through P6 and master schedules, 3–6 week lookaheads, weekly work planning, daily Plan of the Day meetings, subcontractor huddles, field walks, calls, texts, emails, and daily reports. Theia is not based on the assumption that teams do not plan or communicate.

Field reality can change between those planning and synchronization points. One trade runs behind. An inspection does not happen. A delivery misses its window. An area is not ready. One change can affect multiple people and near-term plans.

The product / A live coordination layer for the field

Keep the GC and trades synchronized.

Theia connects near-term plans, progress, constraints, changes, and commitments across the field team. It supports coordination without taking decision authority away from the people closest to the work.

Primary relationship

GC ↔ Sub

Keep the GC and subcontractors aligned on near-term intentions, areas, commitments, progress, readiness, constraints, changes, and what happens next.

GC Super ↔ GC Super

Prevent a change learned by one superintendent or scope from remaining isolated when it affects another superintendent’s work.

Sub ↔ Sub, GC-mediated

Recognize when one trade’s change affects another trade while keeping the GC involved where its judgment or authority is needed.

How Theia works / Plan → reality → response

Six steps around the change.

Theia is a coordination loop for maintaining shared operational awareness as conditions change—not another place to create a master schedule.

  1. Capture plans, commitments, progress, constraints, and changes
  2. Understand what trades intend to do and what is happening
  3. Detect affected people, areas, activities, and commitments
  4. Surface what changed, who is affected, and what matters
  5. Respond with the superintendent’s decision
  6. Synchronize the revised plan and new commitments
Initial productSynchronize + detect impactLater capabilityAssist recovery when reliable information is available

Human-in-the-loop / Field judgment stays central

Your field team makes the call.

Theia supports field judgment; it does not replace it. Experienced superintendents hold context software may not have: actual conditions, safety concerns, means and methods, relationships, contractual considerations, and project-specific realities.

Theia’s roleReduce the work required to gather, connect, relay, and maintain operational information—so the superintendent can make an informed decision and keep the team aligned.

Product philosophy / Clear boundaries

Assist the field team.
Don’t replace its judgment.

Theia is not an AI superintendent, a replacement for the superintendent, another CPM or master scheduling tool, or a promise that every field condition is already known. It is not intended to create another version of the schedule that field teams have to manually maintain.

It does not replace P6 or CPM scheduling.It does not eliminate POD meetings, huddles, field walks, or superintendent judgment.It does not assume every field condition is digitally known.It does not claim to predict every disruption or prove savings before the product exists.

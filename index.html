Theia is an operating system for construction teams. It brings activities, field observations, and project knowledge into a shared workspace.

Capture what happened on site. Review the updates that matter. Ask questions about your project and turn its records into reports. Theia helps your team move from scattered information to a clearer picture of the work.

A little more context

Good questions.

Who is Theia for?

Theia is being built for construction project teams, including superintendents and the people who capture, review, and use information from the field.

What does the AI do?

Theia helps interpret field information and lets you ask questions about project records. Suggested changes need review, and answers depend on the information available. Your team stays responsible for project decisions.

Does Theia replace our scheduling software?

The current workspace focuses on activities, field information, review, and project knowledge. It is not presented as a replacement for a master scheduling tool.
